Tokyo Verdict

Honest gadget guides from Japan.

Tokyo Verdict is a YouTube channel that compares real, named gadgets (earbuds, headphones, phones and more) with prices in US dollars, so you can pick the right one for your budget.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@tokyoverdict

Tokyo Verdict Engine

Tokyo Verdict Engine is the channel's own internal publishing tool. It uses the YouTube API only to manage the Tokyo Verdict channel: uploading and scheduling our videos, setting thumbnails, reading and replying to comments on our videos, and reading the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
