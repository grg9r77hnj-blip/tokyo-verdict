Privacy Policy

Tokyo Verdict Engine · last updated 24 November 2026

Tokyo Verdict Engine ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Tokyo Verdict YouTube channel to manage that channel. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to: upload and schedule videos, set video thumbnails and details, read and reply to comments on Tokyo Verdict videos, and read the channel's own view and subscriber statistics.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Public comments are read only to reply to them on YouTube.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions. Stored data can be deleted by the owner on request.

Contact

Questions: leave a comment on the Tokyo Verdict YouTube channel.
